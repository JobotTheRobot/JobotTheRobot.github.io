Sidekick Automation

A senior design project exploring simple, reconfigurable benchtop automation for repetitive manual manufacturing tasks.

StatusEarly development
RoleTeam member / Customer Discovery Lead
TimelineAugust 2026-present
ToolsCustomer Discovery · Robotics · Mechanical Design · Product Development

Technical details

The concept is a compact, approximately four-degree-of-freedom arm with actuators near the base. The team is exploring hand-guided teaching and interchangeable task-specific modules; these are design directions, not validated features.

Design decisions

Customer conversations highlighted the cost and difficulty of integration, changeovers, and adapting automation to different tasks. I’ve led outreach and interviews to understand the problem before committing to hardware.

Challenges & results

Customer discovery has helped narrow the problem, but a complete robot has not been built or demonstrated. Next is turning findings into a constrained prototype and testing whether the setup is useful.
